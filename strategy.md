# Pulse Analytics Social Strategy

## Objective
Rebuild organic distribution and qualified awareness by making Egyptian retail traders recognize hidden patterns in their own trading.

The feed should make a trader think: "ده بالظبط اللي بيحصل معايا." Then demonstrate why Pulse exists.

## Content pillars
1. Trading self-awareness — what trading history reveals, repeated decisions, process vs outcome.
2. Trading contradictions — profitable result vs poor process, winning trade vs good decision, P&L vs how the result was produced.
3. See Beyond the Numbers — position anatomy, lot/cycle behavior, realized/unrealized results, concentration, entry/exit patterns.
4. Product proof — real Pulse UI; problem -> Pulse reveals -> trader understands.
5. Useful education — only with a relevant Pulse/trader angle.
6. Conversation — genuine trader questions, never empty engagement bait.

## Channel priorities
1. Facebook
2. Instagram
3. X
Secondary: LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube.

Adapt content to each platform; do not blindly duplicate identical copy.

## Cadence
Start around 3-4 substantial pieces/week plus 1-2 short-form pieces where useful. During restart, a daily run may publish a primary or lighter piece. Quality and learning beat volume.

## Creative
Use Pulse UI, data visualizations, annotated charts, carousels, short motion, product demonstrations, and concept graphics derived from the existing visual system. Avoid generic AI imagery, stock photography, blue SaaS templates, neon trading aesthetics, fake terminal screens, and random 3D visuals.

## Performance hierarchy
Prioritize meaningful reach, shares, clicks, follower conversion, meaningful comments, saves, then reactions. Likes alone are not a success metric.

## Learning
Every meaningful post has one hypothesis. Compare against recent comparable posts and use results to choose the next test. Do not declare a winner from a single weak sample.
